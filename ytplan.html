Phase 1 : Market Magic

Why Kids Poetry on YouTube is a Golden Opportunity

Explained simply: How toddlers and algorithms create unstoppable channel growth.

🔄

The Endless Replay Loop

Unlike tech reviews or vlogs that people watch once, toddlers watch their favorite rhymes dozens of times a day! This creates sky-high audience retention and watch time that YouTube's algorithm loves to promote.

🇮🇳

The Hindi Market Boom

Millions of parents across India and the global diaspora look for safe, modern, culturally rich Hindi poems that teach good morals, numbers, and basic concepts with high aesthetic quality.

🚀

Our Test-the-Waters Goal

Our immediate goal is simple: launch our first 10-15 polished poems, test how the YouTube algorithm responds, refine our visual style, and build an organic springboard for massive scaling.

🏆

Real-World Inspiration: How the Giants Did It

Channels like ChuChu TV, Infobells Hindi, and CoComelon styles started with simple, catchy audio paired with vibrant visuals. Here is how they scaled:

⏱️ Timeline to Traction

Most top channels took 12 to 18 months of consistent weekly uploads before hitting hockey-stick viral growth as their catalog compounded in search results.

💰 Monetization Reality

Kids' channels ("Made for Kids" COPPA tag) have lower AdSense rates due to no targeted ads. Real wealth comes from massive view volume + high-ticket brand sponsorships with toy & edtech companies.

🎯 The Winning Recipe

Catchy rhythm + 3-second hook + colorful 3D/2D animation + parent-approved values = viral word-of-mouth sharing in family WhatsApp groups!

Phase 3 : The Partnership Agreement

The 70 / 30 Ownership Framework

Our formal agreement baseline, recorded via email as legal proof before incorporation.

📜 Memorandum of Understanding

Key Terms Summary

🤝

Overall Ownership Split: 70% / 30%

The overall channel ownership (covering future channel valuation, intellectual property rights, and operating revenues) is split as 70% for the Content Creator and 30% for the YouTube Studio.

💳

Subscription & Out-of-Pocket Expenses

Subscription expenses currently borne by the Content Creator will be officially tracked and logged. Once the channel begins generating revenue, these early subscription costs can be reimbursed from initial earnings or factored into valuation calculations.
